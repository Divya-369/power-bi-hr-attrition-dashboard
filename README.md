# HR Attrition & Workforce Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This interactive Power BI dashboard analyzes employee attrition and workforce patterns using the IBM HR Analytics dataset.

The dashboard helps identify employee groups with higher attrition risk and explores factors such as overtime, income, tenure, department, job role, job satisfaction, and work-life balance.

## Dashboard Preview

![HR Attrition Dashboard](./IBM%20HR%20Dashboard.png)

## Key KPIs

- Total Employees: 1,470
- Employees Left: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Average Monthly Income: 6.50K
- Average Years at Company: 7.01

## Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard explores:

- Attrition Rate by Department
- Top 5 Job Roles by Attrition
- Overtime vs Attrition Rate
- Attrition by Tenure Group
- Attrition by Income Group
- Job Satisfaction vs Work-Life Balance

## Key Insights

- Employees working overtime have a substantially higher attrition rate.
- Sales has the highest departmental attrition rate.
- Sales Representatives show the highest attrition among the analyzed job roles.
- Employees with less than one year of tenure have particularly high attrition.
- Lower-income employee groups show elevated attrition levels.
